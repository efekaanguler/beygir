On each turn, first determine which moves satisfy ordinary movement and king safety. If at least one of those moves captures an opposing piece, only those legal captures are allowed. Every move to an empty square is then illegal. A capture that would leave the mover's king in check cannot trigger the requirement. If no legal capture exists, any ordinary legal move is allowed. This restriction is reapplied to the new board on each turn. Attack geometry, including the definition of check, is unchanged.
