Exchange the movement and attack geometry of bishops and knights for both sides. A piece labelled B or b moves, attacks and captures with a knight jump. A piece labelled N or n slides diagonally, and its movement and attack rays are blocked by the first intervening piece. Keep the piece's original label and color when updating the board. Use the changed attack geometry to decide whether a king is in check and whether a move exposes its king. All other pieces keep their ordinary movement, and both captures and moves to empty squares are allowed if legal.
